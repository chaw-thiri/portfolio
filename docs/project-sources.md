The initial review covered documented application and research projects. General course
collections, introductory exercises, the empty salary prediction repository,
the profile and portfolio repositories, and the Bandit repository are not
presented as standalone portfolio projects. The section links to the full
repository collection for additional work.

Descriptions avoid unsupported accuracy, deployment, and production-readiness
claims. In particular, the seismic regression experiment uses dense networks
and reports limited magnitude performance; it is distinct from the USGS
monitoring dashboard. PromptShield remains an experimental research prototype.
FocusFlow's package manifest takes precedence over inconsistent version numbers
in its README; its card uses framework names without version claims.

Demo buttons point to videos or presentations linked from the repositories,
not to assumed live deployments. External playback and sharing permissions are
controlled by the respective hosting services.
